Who it's for

Built for people who run the business, not just use the software

This course is for owners and operational decision-makers at solo and micro businesses (companies with up to 10 employees) who have a genuine operational problem they understand well enough to scope and solve themselves. Each live cohort itself is kept small, 3 to 6 participants, so there's real time for your specific problem.

A good fit if

  • You have a real, recurring operational problem, and a fallback one
  • You're comfortable typing, reading AI-generated code, and testing results, even without a programming background
  • You're willing to work with synthetic or non-sensitive data only
  • You can commit to six 3-hour live sessions plus guided work between them
  • You'll set up ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub, and Streamlit access before Session 1

Not a fit if

  • You want a production system built for you, not to learn how to build one
  • Your project requires live sensitive, confidential, or personal data
  • You're looking for ongoing implementation or private consulting support

The common build

Everyone builds the same starting point: a client inquiry and follow-up tracker

The shared application

Using synthetic data, every participant builds the same AI-assisted client inquiry and follow-up tracker from a starter repository. It calls an authenticated language-model API and a public holiday-checking API, handles a real error, keeps credentials out of the code, and runs in Streamlit. You'll maintain your own private GitHub repository, record a known-good version, deploy it, and demonstrate a rollback.

The starter gives you structure, not the answer. What you build, test, and can explain is what gets assessed.

Your own project

Alongside the common build, you apply the same method to your own genuine business problem: you define it, build it, test it, and explain it. A project that turns out to be unsuitable is narrowed, switched to your fallback problem, or completed through the common exercise instead.

Either way, the required result is a working proof of concept for one complete workflow, not a production system.

How the course works, and what it isn't

We teach the method. You make the decisions.

This is a group Weiterbildung (structured professional development), not individual consulting. You choose and scope your problem, write the requirements, generate and review the code, test it, and decide whether it's ready. HammNet does not build or operate participant-specific production systems.

Within the course

  • Teaching repeatable methods and demonstrating common tasks
  • Asking diagnostic questions and reviewing your evidence
  • Explaining errors and assessing competence

Outside the course

  • Building your specific solution for you
  • Configuring production systems or importing live business data
  • Operating your solution or providing private implementation services
Safety and responsible use

What stays out of the classroom

  • Real sensitive, confidential, or personal data (synthetic or non-sensitive data only)
  • Automated legal, medical, employment, credit, or other high-impact decisions
  • Financial transactions
  • Unsupervised external communications
  • Scraping or automation that could violate a platform's terms
  • Access to production systems where failure could materially harm the business or someone else

Sessions are recorded for the cohort only, kept restricted, and deleted 30 days after the course ends. You'll accept a cohort confidentiality commitment, and camera use is optional.
